Privacy Policy for “Lumena: Find Hidden Objects”

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Data controller: Marcin Korycki, sole proprietor trading as “Marcin Korycki Usługi Programistyczne” (brand: Pixel Corp Studio), ul. Drewnowska 51/66, 91-002 Łódź, Poland, tax ID (NIP) 7272820040, REGON 368656876, email: contact@pixelcorpstudio.com.

1. Scope

This policy covers the Lumena Android app (com.pixelcorpstudio.lumena). The app is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

2. No accounts; data stored on your device

Lumena has no user accounts or sign-in. Your game progress (unlocked levels, medals, time records, language and music settings, purchase status) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads (banner, interstitial, rewarded) served by Google AdMob. To serve and settle them, Google (as an independent controller for advertising purposes) processes, among other things: the Android advertising ID, your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and OS information, and ad impression and click data. Purposes: serving ads, frequency capping, measuring performance, preventing abuse and — if you consent — personalizing ads. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we show a consent form on first launch (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change or withdraw your consent for personalized ads and other purposes at any time in the app's Settings (“Privacy options”) or by emailing us. Without consent to personalization you may see non-personalized ads; under Google's policies they may still use identifiers for, among other things, frequency capping and abuse prevention, but not for personalization.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact location depends on the Android version and manufacturer). Once deleted, the ID is all zeros and ads are not personalized based on it.

The “Remove ads” purchase disables banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain optional.

4. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

With your consent we use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and to improve it (for example, which levels players abandon). Collected data includes in-game events (level start/finish, level result — time, items found and misses, boosts used, ads watched, purchases with product ID and price), a random app-instance ID, language, device model, OS version and approximate location derived from the IP address. We do not collect your name, email address or precise location. Collection stays off until you consent. You can turn analytics off in the app's Settings or by withdrawing consent (see section 3).

In Lumena, Firebase Analytics does not collect the advertising ID or ad-personalization signals; the advertising ID is used only by Google AdMob for ads (section 3).

5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store payment details (e.g., card numbers). We receive confirmation from Google that a product was purchased so we can unlock it and restore it after reinstalling.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — personalized ads and analytics,
  • performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — purchases and operating the game,
  • legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — security, abuse prevention, fixing bugs.

7. Recipients and transfers outside the EEA

Data is processed by Google (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play) as an independent controller or processor under its own terms. Data may be transferred outside the EEA (including to the USA) under GDPR mechanisms (e.g., standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision).

8. Retention

Local data: until you uninstall the app. Firebase analytics data: up to 14 months (set in the Firebase console). Advertising data: per Google's policies.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, object, and withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing). You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Contact us: contact@pixelcorpstudio.com. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (see section 3).

10. Children

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that data was collected from a minor, we will delete it. If you believe this happened, contact us.

11. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app or on this page. The current version is always available at this address.

12. Contact

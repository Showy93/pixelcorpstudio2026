Privacy Policy for “Who Lives Where”

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Data controller: Marcin Korycki, sole proprietor trading as “Marcin Korycki Usługi Programistyczne” (brand: Pixel Corp Studio), ul. Drewnowska 51/66, 91-002 Łódź, Poland, tax ID (NIP) 7272820040, REGON 368656876, e-mail: contact@pixelcorpstudio.com.

1. Scope

This policy covers the Android app “Who Lives Where” (package com.pixelcorpstudio.wholiveswhere), an Einstein-style logic puzzle game. The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

2. No accounts; data stored on your device

The app has no user accounts or sign-in. Your progress (completed levels), settings (language, music, automatic “certain”), purchase status and the counter used to space out ads are stored locally on your device and are not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the app deletes this data. Usage statistics (with your consent) are described in section 4.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads: a banner in the level list, full-screen ads between levels and rewarded ads (shown voluntarily, in exchange for a hint or a check). The ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and account for them, Google (as a separate controller for advertising purposes) processes, among other things, the Android Advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and system information, and ad impression and click data. Purposes: serving ads, frequency capping, measuring effectiveness, preventing abuse and — if you consent — ad personalization. Google’s rules: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we show a consent form on first launch (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app’s Settings (“Privacy options (ads)”, shown when the form is required in your country) or by writing to us. Without consent to personalization you may see non-personalized ads; under Google’s rules they may still use identifiers for purposes such as frequency capping and abuse prevention, but not for personalization.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact location depends on your Android version and device maker). Once deleted, the ID consists of zeros and ads are not personalized based on it.

The “Remove ads” purchase removes the banner and the full-screen ads between levels. Rewarded ads stay voluntary (the “Unlimited hints and checks” purchase removes them).

4. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

With your consent (the UMP form where required) we use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and to improve it (e.g. on which levels players give up). We collect in-game events (e.g. level start and completion, solving time, number of hints and checks used, ads watched and their outcome, purchases with the product ID, changes of settings such as language), a random app-instance ID, language, device model, OS version and approximate location (country/region) derived from the IP address. We do not collect your name, e-mail address, precise location or content from your device. In our game Firebase Analytics does not collect the advertising ID or ad-personalization signals; the advertising ID is used only by AdMob (section 3). Collection is off until you consent, and you can turn analytics off in the game’s Settings (“Analytics”) or by changing your consent under “Privacy options”.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

One-time purchases (“Remove ads”, “Unlimited hints and checks”, “Unlock all levels”) are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details (card number etc.). We receive confirmation from Google that a product was purchased so that we can activate it and restore it after reinstalling the app.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — personalized ads and analytics,
  • performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — processing purchases and running the game,
  • legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — non-personalized ads, security, abuse prevention.

7. Recipients and transfers outside the EEA

Data is processed by Google (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play) as a separate controller or processor, under its own policies. Data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area (including to the USA) under GDPR mechanisms (e.g. standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision).

8. Retention

Local data is kept until you uninstall the app. Analytics data in Firebase is kept for up to 14 months (setting in the Firebase console). Advertising data is retained under Google’s policies. We do not store any user data on our own servers.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your data and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing). You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). Write to us: contact@pixelcorpstudio.com. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (see section 3).

10. Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. If we learn that data was collected from a younger child, we will delete it. If you believe this happened, please write to us.

11. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app or on this page. The current version is always available at this address.

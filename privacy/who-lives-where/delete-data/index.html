How to request deletion of your data — Who Lives Where

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This page applies to the Android app Who Lives Where (com.pixelcorpstudio.wholiveswhere) by Pixel Corp Studio (Marcin Korycki Usługi Programistyczne). The app has no accounts or sign-in and we do not keep your data on our own servers. Below is what you can do and what happens to each kind of data. See also the privacy policy.

1. Data stored on your device (progress, settings, purchase status)

What to do: uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → Who Lives Where → Storage → Clear data.

What is deleted: your progress (completed levels), settings (language, music, automatic “certain”), the counter used to space out ads and the saved purchase status. This data never leaves your device, so nothing remains with us. Purchases stay linked to your Google Play account and can be restored after reinstalling.

2. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

What to do: in Android Settings (usually Privacy → Ads) delete or reset your advertising ID, and in the game open ⚙️ Settings → “Privacy options (ads)” (shown where required) to change or withdraw your consent.

What happens: this data is processed by Google as an independent controller and kept under Google’s policies (policies.google.com/technologies/ads). We cannot delete it on Google’s side.

3. Analytics data (Firebase Analytics, only with your consent)

What to do: turn analytics off in the game: ⚙️ Settings → “Analytics” (no new data is collected afterwards). To ask us to delete the data already collected, email contact@pixelcorpstudio.com with the subject “Delete my data — Who Lives Where” and, if you can, your Android device model and the approximate dates you played.

What is deleted or kept: analytics events are linked only to a random app-instance ID (no name or e-mail). Because of that we may not be able to match them to you, but we will delete what is technically possible within 30 days of your request. Otherwise the data is deleted automatically after 14 months (our retention setting).

4. Purchases (Google Play)

Payments and purchase history are held by Google Play, not by us. To manage or delete them, use your Google Account and Google Play settings. We store only the information that a product was unlocked, on your device.

5. Contact

Pixel Corp Studio (Marcin Korycki Usługi Programistyczne), ul. Drewnowska 51/66, 91-002 Łódź, Poland, e-mail: contact@pixelcorpstudio.com. We respond within 30 days.

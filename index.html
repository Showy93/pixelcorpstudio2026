About the studio

Pixel Corp Studio is a mobile game brand run by Marcin Korycki as a sole proprietorship, Marcin Korycki Usługi Programistyczne (NIP: 7272820040), in business since 2017. The Pixel Corp Studio brand was created in 2026 — our first mobile game, PixelDraw, is now available on Google Play.

Our games

PixelDraw is available on Google Play. Our second game, Lumena, is coming soon.

PixelDraw: Color by Number

A calm puzzle game for Android. Paint the squares by number and a pixel-art picture appears.

  • Tap a square or drag your finger to paint many at once
  • Two difficulty levels, from small pictures to huge, detailed boards
  • Free to play (with ads), works offline
Get it on Google Play

Lumena: Find Hidden Objects

An atmospheric hidden-object game for Android. Explore hand-painted worlds, from a moonlit valley to a sunken temple, and spot the items blended into every scene.

  • Always 5 items to find at a time, each with a faded preview
  • Race the clock for bronze, silver and gold medals and beat your own records
  • Free to play (with ads and optional purchases), works offline, for ages 18+
Coming soon to Google Play
